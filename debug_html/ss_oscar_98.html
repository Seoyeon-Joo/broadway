2025 Academy Award Nominated Screenplays - The 98th Oscars
Held in 2026.

Each year studios will post the screenplays (usually shooting scripts) of films that they are putting up for award consideration for original or adapted screenplay - Academy Awards, Golden Globes, Bafta, etc., aka FYC scripts. For the past 24 years, SimplyScripts has been tracking those scripts as they are posted by the studios. I encourage you to download those you find interesting for personal/educational use. They usually disappear after award season has concluded. Huge thanks to Barksid and Sean and Bojack and 8flix.com for the heads up on so many award season scripts. Here are the 2025-2026 FYC Screenplays.
After The Hunt by Nora Garrett hosted by Amazon MGM Studios

(August 13, 2024 draft) - A college professor finds herself at a personal and professional crossroads when a star pupil levels an accusation against one of her colleagues and a dark secret from her own past threatens to come to light. (pdf) IMDb Info

Belén by Laura Paredes & Dolores Fonzi (Based on the book “Somos Belén”by Anna Correa (Co-Written by Agustina San Martín and Nicolás Britos)) hosted by Amazon MGM Studios

(undated, unspecified draft) - Set in Argentina’s province of Tucumán, BELÉN tells the true story of Julieta, a young woman accused of infanticide, and Soledad Deza, the bold lawyer who risks everything to take on the highly controversial, precedent-setting case. (pdf) IMDb Info

Belén by Laura Paredes and Dolores Fonzi (Sobre versión de: Agustina San Martín and Nicolás Britos (Dirección Dolores Fonzi)) hosted by Amazon MGM Studios

Con el apoyo de su abogado y defensoras de los derechos de la mujer, lucha por la justicia en un caso histórico que podría cambiar vidas. (pdf) IMDb Info
