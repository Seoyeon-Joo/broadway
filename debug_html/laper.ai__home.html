A LETTER

A Letter from the Founder to Screenwriters

To you, who just chose to trust Laper

Hi — to you, who just chose to trust Laper,

I'm Zhao, CEO of Laper,Corp.

A few words about myself. Years ago I was a screenwriter — an unsuccessful one. Then I became a developer and built many projects, big and small — still unsuccessful. When AI came, I made a few apps that did okay, enough to fund a start.

And then came all of this. I gave everything to building Laper.ai.

Because I was once a screenwriter, I know what AI should and shouldn't do. I know the format and the details of a script, and the blueprint a writer holds in their mind for a story. Laper's only goal is to make that blueprint visible — not to take the story out of your hands.

Laper aims to do all the pre-production work for every film that might one day be made. It serves the part of writing that AI can never reach — because writing comes from the deepest feelings of the human heart.

Our users always work at night. It reminds me of my own days as a writer. Maybe one day a film of mine will reach the screen — and then, Laper will be free for you, forever. I know that to get there, I have to take good care of every one of you on the other side of the screen.

Laper moves fast — we ship around twenty improvements almost every week. With you using it, it will become the best screenplay editor there is.

Thank you again for your trust. May you, one day, finish that great script of yours.

ChunXiang Zhao

The Laper founding team
