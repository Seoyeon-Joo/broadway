Scope the 8FLiX Universe. Dive into 13 primary genres that screenplays and TV scripts fall into.

100107

Action

Movies in the action genre are fast-paced and include a lot of action like fight scenes, chase scenes, and slow-motion shots.

Browse

100108

Adventure

Films in the adventure genre usually contain the same basic genre elements as an action movie, with the setting as the critical difference.

Browse

100141

Comedy

Comedy scripts are funny and entertaining. The films in this genre center around a comedic premise.

Browse

100134

Drama

The drama genre features stories with high stakes and many conflicts. They demand that every character and scene move the story forward.

Browse

100119

Fantasy

Screenplays in the fantasy genre feature magical and supernatural elements that do not exist in the real world.

Browse

100162

Horror

Horror films feature elements that leave people with an overwhelming sense of fear and dread.

Browse

100157

Musical

Musical movies weave songs or musical numbers into the narrative to progress the story or further develop the characters.

Browse

100160

Mystery

Mystery films are all about the puzzle and often feature a detective or amateur sleuth trying to solve it.

Browse

100152

Romance

Romances are love stories usually centering around two protagonists exploring some of the elements of relationships.

Browse

100100

Sci-Fi

The sci-fi genre builds worlds and alternate realities filled with imagined elements that don’t exist in the real world.

Browse

100446

Sports

Screenplays in the sports genre will center around a team, individual player, or fan, and with the sport itself advancing the story.

Browse

100159

Thriller

Thrillers expertly blend mystery, tension, and anticipation into one exciting story. Basically nearly every Tom Cruise movie.

Browse

100114

Western

Westerns tell the tale of a cowboy or gunslinger pursuing an outlaw in the Wild West.
